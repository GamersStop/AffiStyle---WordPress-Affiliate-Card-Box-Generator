AffiStyle Privacy Policy

Our commitment to zero-telemetry software design and responsible personal data management.

Effective Date: August 19, 2026 • Revision 2.4

This Privacy Policy outlines how AffiStyle ("we", "our", or "us"), operated under Dreainno Studio, accessible via https://affistyle.shop, collects, manages, and safeguards information in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and international privacy benchmarks.

Zero-Telemetry Architecture Guarantee: AffiStyle software does not harvest, transmit, or monetize reader traffic from websites using our plugin. All affiliate link click metrics are logged and computed locally inside the site owner's WordPress SQL database (wp_affistyle_clicks). No personal IP logs or behavioral beacons are transmitted to external servers.

1. Information We Collect & Why

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver software updates, fulfill commercial licenses, and provide technical assistance:

  • Commercial Order Records: When you purchase AffiStyle Pro, our Merchant of Record (Lemon Squeezy, LLC) securely processes payment information, name, billing address, IP address, and email for transaction settlement and VAT/tax compliance.
  • Cryptographic License Handshake: When activating an AffiStyle Pro license key in WordPress, our licensing endpoint verifies your license key, production site domain, and WordPress version to authenticate your active subscription status.
  • Support Communications: Messages submitted via our contact form or sent directly to admin@affistyle.shop are stored securely to provide requested customer service and technical guidance.

2. Local Data Processing on End-User Websites

The AffiStyle plugin operates with privacy-first engineering standards:

  • Local SQL Storage: Outbound clicks through cloaked URLs increment aggregate integer counters locally. No reader IP addresses, browser fingerprint data, or tracking beacons are collected.
  • Edge Geolocation Routing: Regional store redirects read CDN proxy edge headers (e.g., Cloudflare CF-IPCountry) locally in memory on the server during the redirect handshake without writing persistent tracking cookies.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party providers who adhere to strict data security standards:

  • Lemon Squeezy, LLC (USA): PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant Merchant of Record handling checkout security, global tax calculation, and order delivery.
  • Cloudflare & GitHub Inc. (USA): Secure content delivery network and SSL/TLS encrypted hosting infrastructure.
  • FormSubmit.co: Encrypted, serverless contact form relay forwarding support inquiries directly to our developer inbox.

4. Cookies and Web Storage

Our promotional website (https://affistyle.shop) uses strictly necessary session storage for user preference caching and anonymous aggregated Google Analytics (gtag.js) to evaluate technical performance. We do not sell or trade user data to third-party ad networks.

5. Your Statutory Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under international privacy regulations, you have the statutory right to:

  • Request access to personal data associated with your order account.
  • Request rectification of inaccurate contact information.
  • Request permanent deletion of your licensing customer records upon cancellation.
  • Opt out of any marketing or promotional communications.

6. Data Protection Inquiries

To exercise your statutory data privacy rights or ask questions regarding our privacy architecture, please contact our Data Protection desk directly:

  • Email: admin@affistyle.shop
  • Operating Studio: Dreainno Studio (dreainno.online)
