Refund & Cancellation Policy

We stand firmly behind our code. If AffiStyle Pro isn't the right fit for your website, we make refunds straightforward and hassle-free.

🛡️ 14-Day 100% Money-Back Guarantee Active

At AffiStyle, we want every WordPress creator and publisher to succeed with high-converting, compliant product showcases. If you purchase AffiStyle Pro and determine that it does not fit your editorial workflow or experiences technical theme incompatibilities that we cannot resolve, we provide a full 100% refund within 14 calendar days of your checkout timestamp.

Our Promise: No hidden conditions, no forced phone calls, and no runarounds. If our engineering team cannot resolve a technical conflict or if you decide it's not for you within 14 days, you get your money back.

How to Request a Refund in 3 Simple Steps

1

Send Quick Email

Send an email to admin@affistyle.shop with the subject "Refund Request - [Order Number]".

2

Provide Order ID

Include your Lemon Squeezy Order Number and a brief note describing the reason or theme incompatibility.

3

Fast Reimbursement

Our team approves and executes the refund via Lemon Squeezy within 24–48 business hours back to your card.

1. Eligibility Criteria

  • 14-Day Window: Your refund request must be submitted within fourteen (14) calendar days from the date and time of the initial license purchase.
  • Product Covered: This policy applies to all new commercial license purchases of AffiStyle Pro processed through our official checkout.
  • Support Opportunity: While optional, we encourage you to allow our developer desk an opportunity to assist with any WordPress theme styling conflicts, as most CSS layout adjustments can be patched within a few hours.

2. Processing Time & Method

All financial refunds are issued directly through our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy, LLC:

  • Once approved, refunds are initiated immediately and typically reflect on your bank or credit card statement within 3 to 7 business days, depending on your card issuer.
  • The full purchase price is refunded in the original currency of transaction.

3. License Deactivation Post-Refund

Upon completion of a refund:

  • Your commercial license key will be cryptographically revoked from our update server.
  • Any existing cards on your site using Pro templates (Styles 3–16) can gracefully revert to open-source Style 1 or Style 2 cards.
  • Your data, product details, and saved affiliate links remain 100% safe in your local WordPress database without loss.

4. Subscription Renewals & Cancellations

AffiStyle Pro licenses renew automatically on an annual basis to ensure uninterrupted security updates and developer support. You can cancel auto-renewal at any time with a single click inside your Lemon Squeezy Customer Portal or by emailing admin@affistyle.shop.
