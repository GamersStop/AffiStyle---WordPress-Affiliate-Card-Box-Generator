Free 1. Installation & Setup

AffiStyle Core is 100% free open-source software under GPL-2.0, hosted on the official WordPress.org plugin directory.

  1. Log in to your WordPress Admin Dashboard.
  2. Navigate to Plugins → Add New Plugin.
  3. Search for AffiStyle in the search bar.
  4. Click Install Now, and once installed, click Activate.
  5. A new Affiliate Boxes menu item will appear in your admin sidebar.

Free 2. Creating Your First Affiliate Card

Each affiliate card is configured once in your admin dashboard and can be reused or embedded across hundreds of posts simultaneously.

  1. Go to Affiliate Boxes → Add New Box.
  2. Enter a descriptive admin title (e.g., "Sony WH-1000XM5 Headphone Card").
  3. In the Product 1 Details section, fill in:
    • Product Title: Customer-facing headline name.
    • Product Image: Select from your WordPress Media Library or upload a high-resolution image. Explicit aspect ratios are auto-calculated for 0 CLS.
    • Description: 2–3 concise sentences highlighting key features.
    • Affiliate Link: Your destination affiliate URL (Amazon, ShareASale, CJ, Impact, etc.).
    • CTA Button Text: e.g., "Check Price on Amazon" or "Buy Directly →".
    • Star Rating & Badges: Numeric score (e.g. 4.8 / 5.0) and optional badge (e.g. "Editor's Choice").
    • Pros & Cons List: Add bullet points highlighting product strengths and drawbacks.
  4. Under Template Style, choose Style 1 (Standard Box) or Style 2 (Compact Card).
  5. Click Publish.

Free 3. Inserting Cards into Content

AffiStyle supports both native Gutenberg blocks and universal shortcodes:

  • Gutenberg Native Block: In any post or page, click the + inserter, search for Affiliate Box, and select your published box from the live preview dropdown.
  • Universal Shortcode: Copy the box shortcode from the admin table and paste it anywhere: 
    [aff_box id="123"]
    Works seamlessly with Elementor, Divi, Beaver Builder, GeneratePress, Kadence, and the Classic Editor.

Free 4. Link Cloaking & Automated FTC Compliance

AffiStyle protects both your SEO rankings and legal compliance automatically:

  • Branded Outbound URLs: Raw affiliate links are cleanly masked under your domain: 
    https://yourdomain.com/recommends/sony-headphones/
    Using safe HTTP 302 redirects with no visitor cookies set.
  • Automated FTC Disclosures: A compliant micro-disclosure ("We may earn an affiliate commission when you purchase through links on our site") is appended beneath each card.
  • SEO Safe Links: All button links enforce rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" and open in a new tab.

Pro 5. Installing & Activating Pro

AffiStyle Pro unlocks commercial capabilities for high-traffic niche sites.

  1. Make sure AffiStyle Free is active on your site.
  2. Download affistyle-pro.zip from your Lemon Squeezy order confirmation email.
  3. Go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, select the ZIP file, and click Activate Plugin.
  4. Navigate to Affiliate Boxes → License Settings.
  5. Paste your Lemon Squeezy License Key and click Activate License.
Cryptographic Security: Pro licenses are verified via HMAC-SHA256 signatures, ensuring immediate activation and background updates directly in your WP dashboard.

Pro 6. Unlocking 16+ Designer Templates

Once Pro is active, the style selector unlocks all 16 layouts:

  • Styles 3–8: Minimalist Accent Grids, Floating Cards, and Glassmorphism.
  • Styles 9–13: Modern Dark Mode, Gradient Borders, and 3-Column Tiered Showcases.
  • Styles 14, 15 & 16: Side-by-Side Versus Battle Cards, Showdown Duels, and Spec Comparison Tables.

Pro 7. Side-by-Side Versus Battle Cards

Pit two products against each other in a direct showdown card:

  1. Select Style 14 (Versus Duel) or Style 15 (Spec Matrix).
  2. Toggle on [x] Enable Product 2 Comparison.
  3. Fill in Product 2 details: Title, Image, Affiliate URL, Rating, and Pros/Cons.
  4. Both products render side-by-side with independent click tracking on mobile and desktop.

Pro 8. Multi-Retailer Choice Buttons

Give buyers multiple merchant options to boost conversion rates by up to 38%:

  1. Under Alternative Retailer Buttons, click + Add Retailer.
  2. Add Button 1: Amazon (e.g. $299), Button 2: Best Buy (e.g. $299), Button 3: Direct Store.
  3. Customize button colors, icons, and affiliate tracking parameters per store.

Pro 9. Global Geo-Targeted Store Routing

Stop losing international commissions. Automatically route clicks to localized Amazon or merchant stores:

  1. Open the Geo-Targeted Store Routing panel.
  2. Enter regional links for US, UK, CA, and IN.
  3. AffiStyle reads Cloudflare edge headers (CF-IPCountry) locally in memory and routes each visitor to their regional store instantly.

10. Performance Benchmarks & 0.000 CLS Impact

How AffiStyle maintains 100/100 Google PageSpeed scores:

  • Under 15KB Total Footprint: Zero jQuery, zero heavy external runtime scripts.
  • Conditional Asset Loading: CSS and JS are only enqueued on posts where an affiliate box is rendered.
  • Aspect-Ratio Containment: Prevents unrendered container expansions and layout shifts.
