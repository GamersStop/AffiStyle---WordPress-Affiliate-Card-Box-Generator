AffiStyle Complete Documentation

Comprehensive Guide • Free & Pro Editions

Welcome to the official developer and user guide for AffiStyle. This document provides complete instructions for configuring both the Free Edition and the Pro Commercial Edition.

Part 1: Free Edition Guide

1. Installation & Setup (Free)

  1. Log in to your WordPress Admin dashboard.
  2. Navigate to Plugins → Add New.
  3. Search for AffiStyle in the repository search bar.
  4. Click Install Now, then click Activate.

2. Creating Your First Affiliate Card

  1. Go to Affiliate Boxes → Add New Box in your WordPress sidebar.
  2. Enter a descriptive internal title for reference (e.g., "Sony WH-1000XM5 Review Box").
  3. In the Product 1 Details section, populate:
    • Product Title: The customer-facing product name.
    • Product Image: Upload or select from your WordPress Media Library.
    • Description: A brief 2–3 sentence review summary.
    • Default Affiliate URL: Your raw affiliate or partner link.
    • Button Text: Custom CTA copy (e.g., "Check Price on Amazon").
    • Badge / Ribbon Text: Optional highlight badge (e.g., "Best Overall Pick").
    • Star Rating & Review Count: Numerical score from 0.0 to 5.0 and optional review total.
    • Pros & Cons: Enter positive points and negative points separated by new lines.
  4. Under Card Styling, choose between Style 1 (Standard Box) or Style 2 (Compact Minimalist), and optionally set custom background and button colors.
  5. Click Publish.

3. Inserting Cards into Content

  • Gutenberg Block: In any post or page, click the + inserter, search for Affiliate Box, and select your published card from the dropdown preview list.
  • Shortcode: Copy the generated shortcode (e.g., [aff_box id="123"]) and paste it into Elementor, Divi, Beaver Builder, or Classic Editor widgets.

4. Link Cloaking & FTC Compliance

  • Branded Outbound URLs: All affiliate destination links are automatically cloaked under yoursite.com/recommends/your-card-slug/ with safe 302 redirects.
  • FTC Disclosure: A legally compliant micro-disclosure is automatically attached beneath the box, and all CTA links have rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" and target="_blank" applied.
Part 2: Pro Commercial Edition Guide

1. Installing & Activating Pro

  1. Ensure AffiStyle Free is installed and activated on your WordPress site.
  2. Download the affistyle-pro.zip package from your Lemon Squeezy receipt email.
  3. Navigate to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin and upload the ZIP archive.
  4. Click Activate Plugin.
  5. Go to Affiliate Boxes → Settings / License, paste your Lemon Squeezy License Key, and click Activate License. Your license will verify cryptographically via HMAC-SHA256.

2. Unlocking 16+ Premium Templates

Once Pro is active, the Template Style dropdown unlocks all 16 layouts:

  • Styles 3–13: Modern Dark Cards, Floating Glassmorphism, Gradient Accent Boxes, and 3-Column Tiered Grids.
  • Styles 14, 15 & 16: Side-by-Side Versus Battle Cards, Comparison Showdowns, and Feature Spec Matrix Tables.

3. Configuring Side-by-Side Showdown Cards (Product 1 vs Product 2)

  1. Open an Affiliate Box and choose a comparison template (e.g. Style 14 Versus or Style 15 Spec Matrix).
  2. Check the [x] Enable Product 2 Comparison toggle.
  3. Fill in Product 2 details: Title, Image, Description, URL, Badge (e.g. "Runner Up"), Star Rating, and Pros/Cons.
  4. Both products will render side-by-side with independent click tracking (data-product="1" and data-product="2").

4. Multi-Retailer CTA Buttons (Choice Architecture)

  1. In the Pro editor, expand the Alternative Retailers & Stores repeater.
  2. Click + Add Store Button to add secondary buttons (e.g., Button 1: Amazon, Button 2: Best Buy, Button 3: Walmart).
  3. Assign custom button labels, localized affiliate URLs, price tags (e.g., "$299"), and custom button colors.

5. Global Geo-Targeted Regional Link Routing

  1. Expand the Geo-Targeted Store Routing panel.
  2. Enter regional store URLs for your target international markets (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and India).
  3. AffiStyle Pro detects visitor country codes via CDN edge headers (Cloudflare CF-IPCountry) and automatically redirects cloaked clicks to the appropriate regional merchant link.

6. Custom Cloak Prefixes & HTTP Status Codes

In Affiliate Boxes → Pro Settings, customize your site-wide cloak prefix from /recommends/ to /go/, /out/, or /deal/, and configure HTTP redirect status codes between 302 (Temporary), 301 (Permanent), or 307 (Temporary Redirect).
